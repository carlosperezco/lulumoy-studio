Bilateral filter

Smooths uniform areas without blurring edges: each neighbour is weighted by its distance and by how similar its value is to the centre pixel.

Parameters

ParameterMeaning
Neighbourhood diameterd, neighbourhood diameter in pixels. Large values (above 9) are slow.
Sigma colourσc: intensity difference beyond which two pixels "do not look alike". Large = behaves like a Gaussian.
Sigma spaceσs: spatial reach of the average, in pixels.

Note: Only 8-bit images, grayscale or colour without alpha.
