Perspective transform (4 points)

Homography: the mapping between two views of a plane taken from different points. It preserves straight lines but not parallelism (parallel lines may converge to a vanishing point). Useful to straighten documents, façades or boards photographed at an angle.

As with the affine transform, cv::warpPerspective computes each destination pixel with the inverse transform H−1 and interpolates it. The result keeps the image size.

Parameters

ParameterMeaning
InterpolationHow values at non-integer positions are computed.
Empty areasValue of pixels that fall outside the original image.
