Notation

I(x, y) is the value of the pixel in column x and row y, with the origin at the top-left corner; I′ is the result. W×H is the image size and M the maximum value of its bit depth (255 for 8 bits, 65535 for 16 bits). ∗ denotes convolution. Unless stated otherwise, colour images are processed channel by channel.
