Gaussian blur

Weighted average of the neighbours using a Gaussian bell: it attenuates noise and fine detail, giving more weight near the centre.

Parameters

ParameterMeaning
Kernel size (odd)k, side of the kernel. It must be odd: an even value is raised to the next odd one. With a fixed sigma, k ≈ 6σ + 1 avoids truncating the bell.
Sigma (0 = automatic)σ in pixels: the larger, the stronger the blur. 0 = computed from k.
