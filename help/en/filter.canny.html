Canny edges

Canny edge detector: finds the lines of maximum gradient and decides which are edges with two thresholds. The result is a binary (0 or 255) grayscale image.

Non-maximum suppression: a pixel remains a candidate only if its |∇I| is a maximum along direction θ, which thins edges to one pixel.

Parameters

ParameterMeaning
Lower thresholdT1, low threshold.
Upper thresholdT2, high threshold. A ratio T2/T1 between 2 and 3 usually works well.
Sobel apertureSize of the Sobel operator (3, 5 or 7). Larger apertures give larger gradients, so thresholds must rise accordingly.

Note: The image is first converted to 8-bit grayscale.
